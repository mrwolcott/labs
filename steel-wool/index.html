The bench

Drag from the steel wool to the scale or a plate to pull off a piece. Tap a piece on the scale to tear a little back off; drag a piece back to the steel wool to put it away. Tap a sample on a plate to fluff it or press it together before it gets wet, and to look at it closely. Drag a sample into the vinegar and hold it there to dip it, onto the paper towel to blot it, and over the sink and hold to shake it. Drag the teaspoon from the saltwater onto a sample to add a level teaspoon. Drag the spray bottle over a sample and hold to wet it. Drag a sample into the jar, and tap the lid to close or open it. Drag samples onto the foil squares, drag the tray by its handle into the oven and back out, and tap the knob to turn the oven on or off. Tap a foil square to tip its loose bits onto the scale. Tap the scale's zero pad to zero it, or its platform to wake it.

Your lab document has the steps for each day. This page keeps your samples on this device between visits, and the eight days run on the real calendar, so come back each day the same way you would check real plates.
