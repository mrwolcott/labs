This bench is for students who could not get hold of the materials. If you can build the real apparatus, do that instead. The real version is the better experience and the one we prefer. Two bowls on the floor

Both wide bowls sit side by side on the same flat surface. The bowls are 20 cm across and 8 cm deep.

Drag the jug over a bowl and hold to pour. Drag a bowl to the sink to empty it. Drag the dry tube onto the bowls to put one end in each and let go; drag it back off to take it out.

There is no sink step for filling the tube in Part A; the tube goes in dry. When you have written down and screenshotted what you saw, open the Parts B and C tab. The bowls there are a separate pair on the bench, so what you poured here does not carry over.

The bench

Drag the jug over a bowl and hold to pour. Drag the receiver bowl over the source and hold to pour it back; drag either bowl to the sink to empty it. Drag books and the stool from the shelf under the source bowl, or drag them back off. Drag the tube to the sink to fill it, then onto the bowls to put the ends in. Drag the top of the tube to hang it higher or lower; drag it to the sink to lift it out. Tap the clamp to open or close it. Tap the scale's zero pad to zero it, or its platform to wake it. Tap a water surface or the top of the tube to look at it against the tape.

Part B and Part C both run on this bench. The tube starts out lying beside the bowls; fill it in the sink before it goes in. For Part C, leave the bowls where they are and drag the top of the tube to hang it. Your lab document has the steps for each trial; the scale, stopwatch, and tape are below and on the scene.
