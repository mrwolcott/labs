Virtual Lab Bench

This bench is for students who could not get hold of the materials. If you can build the real apparatus, do that instead. The real version is the better experience and the one we prefer. Keep your lab document open next to this page and record every reading there, exactly as you would at a real bench. Nothing here is checked, corrected, or scored.

Start your session

Your name and today's date set up your bench. The code at the top right of the screen belongs to your session. Include a screenshot that shows it in your lab report where the name and date photo would otherwise go.

Choose your tubing

Three packages are on the table. Read the label the way you would in a store. Tap one to take it to the bench. You can swap at any time; swapping empties the tube.

No tubing on the bench yet.

Two bowls on the floor

Both wide bowls sit side by side on the same flat surface. The bowls are 20 cm across and 8 cm deep. Hold a pour button to add colored water.

The air-filled tube

The tube is dry and full of air. Put one end into each bowl, over the rims, and let go. Watch for at least thirty seconds.
