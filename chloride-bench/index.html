The bench

Drag the jug over the cups and hold to pour; the longer you hold, the faster it pours. Drag the cups over the sink and hold to pour some off, or let go over the sink to rinse and dry them. Drag the cups or the dish onto the scale or back onto the counter. Tap the scale's ZERO pad to zero it, or its platform to wake it.

Dip the spoon into a container to take a scoop. Hold a loaded spoon over the dish to tap it out a little at a time; let go over the dish to empty it there, or over a container to put it back. Dip an empty spoon into the dish to take a little back out. Rub the glass back and forth over the dish to crush what is in it. Let go of the dish over the cups to add what is in it. Move the spoon around inside the cups to stir. Drag the thermometer into the cups, and back to its holder to take it out. Tap the side of the cups to feel them.
