Before you film

Set the camera square to the motion, keep the path parallel to the lens, film in landscape, and do not move or zoom once recording starts. Put your measuring stick the same distance from the camera as the thing you are measuring. Film at 60 fps if your phone offers it.

If a clip will not load, it is almost always recorded in HEVC. On iPhone open Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible. On Android turn off high efficiency video in the camera app settings, then film the trial again.

These tools read your file straight into the page on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere, so your clips stay on your phone or laptop. Trouble with a tool goes to Mr Wolcott.
