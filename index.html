Arrow keys step one frame. Comma and period work too.

This tool measures motion in the plane of the video. It is right only when the whole motion stays the same distance from the camera. Set the camera square to the path, keep the path parallel to the camera, film in landscape, and do not move or zoom the camera once recording starts. Motion toward or away from the camera reads as smaller than it is, and no amount of careful clicking recovers it.

Step 2 · Scale

Click the two ends of your measuring stick in the frame, then type its real length.

Put the measuring stick in the same plane as the motion, the same distance from the camera as the thing you are marking. A stick nearer or further than the path scales every later measurement wrongly.

Step 3 · Marking

Set the scale first, then click the object once on each frame.

One click makes one point, and the view steps forward a frame on its own. If you miss, delete the point and click again.
