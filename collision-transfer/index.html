Virtual Lab Bench

This bench is for students who could not get hold of the materials. If you can build the real setup, do that instead. The real version is the better experience and the one we prefer. Keep your lab document open next to this page and record every reading there, exactly as you would at a real bench. Nothing here is checked, corrected, or scored.

Start your session

Your name and today's date set up your bench. The code at the top right of the screen belongs to your session. Include a screenshot that shows it in your lab report where the name and date photo would otherwise go.

The table

Drag a marble or a target onto the scale to weigh it, and back to the shelf. Drag a target onto the table to set it at the tape line, and drag it back there after each trial. Tap a target sitting on the tape line to turn it around. Drag the tape marker along the ramp to set the release point, and tap it to read its height. Drag a marble onto the ramp and let go to release it from the marker. Tap a target after it slides to read its position on the ruler. A marble stuck in the putty comes off when you drag it. Tap the scale's zero pad to zero it.

Your lab document has the steps and the order of the parts. Each target on the shelf already has a putty pad on one face. After a bounce, the striker rolls back and comes to rest beside the bottom of the ramp, out of the way, so it cannot strike the target a second time.
