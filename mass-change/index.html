The kitchen

Drag from the foil roll to an empty spot on the counter to fold a tray. Tap a tray's tag to write a label on it. Drag a tray onto the scale to weigh it, and back to the counter or onto the baking sheet. Drag the spoon from the baking soda box onto a tray to add a spoonful; tap the powder in a tray on the scale to tap a little back off. Drag a tray to the trash to empty it; drag an empty tray there to throw it away. Drag the baking sheet by its handle into the oven and back out onto the heat-safe board. Drag the bowl over the baking sheet to cover it, and drag it off to lift it. Use the oven's buttons to turn it on and set the temperature. Tap a tray to look at it closely.

Your lab document has the steps. Every wait on this bench runs on the real clock, so use the timer the way you would at home. You can leave this page open in the background or close it and come back later in the same browser; your trays stay where you left them.
